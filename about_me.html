About

Hi, I'm Kathryn.

I'm a UX/UI designer and front-end developer who likes working at the point where design decisions and code meet. My favorite part of building software isn't just writing it — it's making the experience feel obvious to the person using it.

Background

My background in computer science introduced me to software engineering, user experience, and interactive development. Through coursework and personal projects, I found that the work I'm drawn to isn't the code on its own — it's creating experiences that feel intuitive and considered for the people using them.

I'm based in Phoenix, Arizona, and I'm open to relocation for the right opportunity. I'm currently open to entry-level UX/UI design, front-end development, and design engineering roles.

What I do

I work end to end: research and problem framing, interface design, prototyping, usability testing, and the front-end build that follows. I care most about work where the interface is the product — software people have to use rather than look at.

  • UX research & usability testing
  • UI design & design systems
  • Prototyping in Figma
  • Front-end development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • Accessibility & inclusive design
  • Game & interaction design
